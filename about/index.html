About Dagoldol

Dagoldol Trading Co. is a small online catalogue focused on everyday goods that may be sold by length, standard size, or square meter, depending on the product.

How the shop works

The public catalogue is open for browsing without an account. Product cards show available options, pricing, stock status, brands, ratings, bundles, and current offers when those records are available.

An account is requested only when a customer continues to checkout, views order history, edits a profile, or uses direct messaging.

Product options

The current application supports three measurement models: feet for length-based goods, named sizes such as S–XXL, and square meters. Each product can have its own per-option price, stock level, and image.

Customer support

Questions can be sent through the in-app Contact form or by email. See the Contact page for the current public contact channel.
