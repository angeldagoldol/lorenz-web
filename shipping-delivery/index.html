Shipping & Delivery

Delivery charges are calculated during checkout from the address entered by the customer. The rules below reflect the current application logic.

Current delivery-fee rules

  • Free delivery applies when the road distance is within 5 km of the configured shop origin.
  • The application also checks configured free-delivery zones around Katipunan National High School in Arakan, Cotabato and Kimasog in Marilog District; an address within 5 km of either configured zone receives free delivery.
  • Outside the free-delivery rule, the configured rate is ₱60 per calculated road kilometer.
  • If the application cannot geocode the address or obtain a road route, it uses a ₱600 fallback delivery fee.

Address processing

The checkout sends the entered address to the OpenStreetMap Nominatim geocoding service and uses the public OSRM routing service to estimate road distance. Add a clear landmark because map services may locate a street without identifying the exact door.

Estimated delivery and real status

The current customer-facing delivery estimate is 3–6 days after order placement. Phase 3 no longer advances fulfillment stages automatically from elapsed time: Order Placed, Processing, Shipped, Out for Delivery, and Delivered now reflect the persisted status selected by the seller/admin.

Questions about delivery

If the calculated fee or location looks incorrect, contact Dagoldol before confirming payment. Use the Contact page.
