Terms of Use & Ordering

These terms describe how the current Dagoldol website is intended to be used. They should be read together with the Privacy, Delivery, and Returns pages.

Public catalogue and accounts

You may browse the public catalogue without an account. Checkout, order history, profile features, and direct messages require authentication. You are responsible for keeping your login credentials secure and for providing accurate account and delivery information.

Product information and availability

Products can have option-specific prices, stock levels, images, brands, flash-sale pricing, and bundle pricing. Availability is rechecked during checkout because stock can change between browsing and order submission.

Payment

The current checkout displays GCash and bank-transfer instructions and requires a payment reference before order confirmation. An optional half-payment setting may be available, with the remaining balance shown as due on delivery.

Delivery and promotions

Delivery charges are calculated from the entered address using the rules described on the Shipping & Delivery page. Promotion eligibility depends on the active code, expiry, usage limit, minimum spend, and configured discount.

Cancellation and returns

Customer cancellation is offered before the Shipped stage. Return handling after shipment is not automated; use the Returns & Cancellations page and contact Dagoldol for the specific order.

Acceptable use

Do not attempt to access another customer’s orders, messages, profile data, payment proof, administrator functions, or database records. Do not manipulate request data to alter prices, inventory, promotions, roles, or order status.
