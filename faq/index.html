Frequently Asked Questions

These answers describe the behavior implemented by the current Dagoldol application.

Shopping and accounts

Do I need an account to browse?

No. The catalogue and product information are available to guests. Login is requested when you continue to checkout or use account features.

How are products measured?

Products can be configured by feet, standard size labels, or square meters. The available options are shown before adding or ordering an item.

Can I keep items in my cart before logging in?

Yes. The current guest cart is saved in your browser and is merged into your account cart after a successful login.

Orders and payment

What payment methods are shown in checkout?

The current checkout supports GCash and bank transfer. It also includes an optional half-payment setting where the remaining balance is due on delivery.

Is a payment reference required?

Yes. The current checkout requires a payment reference number before the order can be confirmed. A payment screenshot can also be uploaded.

Can I cancel an order?

The current application allows customer cancellation while an order is still before the Shipped stage. Once it has reached Shipped or later, the cancellation control is no longer offered.

When can I rate a product?

The rating control appears after the order is marked Delivered. Phase 3 also requires the rating to be tied to the authenticated customer, delivered order, and purchased product at the database-policy level.

Delivery

How is the delivery fee calculated?

The checkout geocodes the entered address and requests road distance. The configured rule provides free delivery within the free-distance threshold and otherwise applies the configured per-kilometer rate. If the address cannot be pinpointed, the checkout uses the configured fallback fee.
